# Bourse releases

Signed Android builds of Bourse: your stocks, and a whole market on one map.
Free, no ads.
Installed copies update themselves from here. In a browser:
https://bourse.matisseduffield.workers.dev

**[Download Bourse 1.0.8](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/matisseduffield/bourse-releases/main/Bourse.apk)**

On your phone: open the link, then open the downloaded file and allow
the install when Android asks. You only do this once; after that the app
offers updates itself.

## What's new in 1.0.8

- The market map no longer shows Alphabet, Fox and News Corp twice their size
- An open stock keeps its price and today's chart up to date
- Prices and stats stay fully visible with large text
