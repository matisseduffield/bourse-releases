# Bourse releases

Signed Android builds of Bourse: your stocks, and a whole market on one map.
Free, no ads.
Installed copies update themselves from here. In a browser:
https://bourse.matisseduffield.workers.dev

**[Download Bourse 1.0.13](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/matisseduffield/bourse-releases/main/Bourse.apk)**

On your phone: open the link, then open the downloaded file and allow
the install when Android asks. You only do this once; after that the app
offers updates itself.

## What's new in 1.0.13

- The change under the price now follows the range you pick: past week, past month and so on
